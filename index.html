About Dr. Rahul (PT)

Dr. Rahul (PT) is a qualified physiotherapist, lecturer, and clinical rehabilitation specialist based in Jaipur, Rajasthan. He is the founder and chief physiotherapist at JPCRI (Jaipur Physio & Cardio Rehab Institute), with a focused interest in cardiac rehabilitation, orthopedic rehabilitation, and biomechanics.

Dr. Rahul completed his Bachelor’s degree in Physiotherapy and is currently pursuing advanced specialization in cardiopulmonary physiotherapy. Alongside clinical practice, he serves as a lecturer in biomechanics and physiotherapy subjects, training future physiotherapists with a strong foundation in clinical reasoning and evidence-based practice.

With experience in hospital-based rehabilitation and home-based physiotherapy services, Dr. Rahul has worked with patients suffering from heart attack, high blood pressure, diabetes-related cardiac complications, post-surgical orthopedic conditions, chronic pain, and mobility disorders. His approach focuses on scientific exercise therapy, patient education, and long-term lifestyle modification to ensure sustainable recovery.

Dr. Rahul is passionate about building awareness of cardiac rehabilitation in India, where many heart patients remain unaware that structured physiotherapy can significantly reduce the risk of future cardiac events. Through JPCRI, he aims to make cardiac and pulmonary rehabilitation accessible, affordable, and evidence-based for the community.

Apart from clinical practice, Dr. Rahul is an educator and content creator, producing educational content on physiotherapy, biomechanics, and rehabilitation sciences. His long-term vision is to establish a recognized physiotherapy education platform and contribute to the development of modern rehabilitation practices in India.

    Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

    Is exercise safe after heart attack?

    Yes. Under supervised cardiac rehabilitation, exercise is safe and helps improve heart function, stamina, and reduces risk of future cardiac events.

    When should I start physiotherapy after angioplasty or bypass surgery?

    Physiotherapy and cardiac rehab can start early under medical guidance. Early rehabilitation improves recovery and quality of life.

    Can physiotherapy reduce blood pressure?

    Yes. Regular supervised exercise, breathing techniques, and lifestyle modification can significantly help in blood pressure control.

    How long is a cardiac rehabilitation program?

    Typically 6–12 weeks, depending on patient condition, with long-term maintenance programs recommended.
